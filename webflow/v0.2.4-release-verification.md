## Published checks

Method: Chrome on the published `webflow.io` pages. The automation tab reported `visibilityState: hidden` with no animation frames, so transitions could not advance and window resizing had no effect. Breakpoints were therefore tested in same-origin iframes of the published page at exact widths. End-state geometry was measured with a test-only `transition: none` style inside the iframe. Reduced motion was simulated by injecting the delivered stylesheet's own `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` rules unconditionally inside the iframe. The JavaScript `matchMedia` reduced-motion branch is covered by the automated suite, not this browser run.

### SB Test Five linked `SmashBurger CDN` (`/smashburger-cdn-import-proof`; Mobile landscape, Dropdown, three lines, Show backdrop on)

- First publish of the site, made with Michael's approval for this check.
- Runtime recognised the namespaced Library marker `data-wf--mwp-component-library--smashburger-cdn--variant="mobile-landscape"`.
- Collapse boundary: expanded at 1280, 991, 800 and 768px; collapsed at 767, 700, 479 and 390px. Trigger display, `data-mwp-collapsed` and panel `inert` matched each state; zero horizontal overflow throughout.
- At 600px: opening set `aria-expanded="true"`, removed `inert` and `aria-hidden`; Escape closed the menu, restored `inert` and returned focus to the trigger.
- Icon auto-shift measured `7px` (2px bars, 5px gap). Open three-line X: all bar centres at (12, 8), translations ±7px, middle bar opacity 0. Two-line X: centres converge at y = 4.5, translations ±3.5px, middle bar `display: none`.
- Backdrop: Webflow emitted `data-backdrop="True"`; the Dropdown backdrop reached opacity 1 and became visible when open.
- Motion: normal open panel `0.28s, 0.28s, 0s`, item `0.28s`. Reduced motion: root effective open duration `.01ms`; open panel `0.01ms, 0.01ms, 0s`, item `0.01ms` with `0s` delay, line and backdrop `0.01ms`. This confirms the `v0.2.3` panel/item cascade defect is fixed in the distributed CSS.
